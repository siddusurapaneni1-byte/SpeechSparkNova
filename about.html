About SpeechSpark NOVA

Who we are, why we started, and where we're going.

Our Story

SpeechSpark NOVA was founded by a group of students and educators in Northern Virginia who saw a gap: too many young people struggled to speak in front of others, not because they lacked ideas, but because they lacked practice and support.

We started small — a single camp, a handful of campers, and a big belief that speaking is a skill anyone can learn. That belief hasn't changed. We're growing our programming, building community partnerships, and working toward formal nonprofit registration so we can serve even more students.

🎙️

Mission & Values

Confidence

We create safe, supportive spaces where every camper feels free to try, stumble, and grow without fear of judgment.

Clarity

We teach structured thinking and organized expression — helping campers say exactly what they mean.

Community

We are rooted in Northern Virginia and committed to serving local families, schools, and organizations.

Courage

Speaking in front of others takes bravery. We honor that courage in every camper, every time.

Our Nonprofit Status

⚠️ SpeechSpark NOVA is not yet a registered nonprofit organization.

We are actively working through the process of formal registration. In the meantime, we operate as a community initiative committed to transparency and responsible stewardship of any resources entrusted to us.

If you have questions about our organizational status, please contact us directly.
