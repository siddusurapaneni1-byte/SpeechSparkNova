Our Camps

Upcoming speaking camps across Northern Virginia. All skill levels welcome.

What to Expect

01

Welcome & Icebreakers

Every camp kicks off with fun, low-stakes speaking activities so campers feel comfortable right away.

02

Skill-Building Sessions

Focused workshops on body language, vocal variety, structure, storytelling, and persuasion.

03

Practice Rounds

Campers give multiple speeches with guided feedback from coaches and peers.

04

Final Showcase

Each session ends with a showcase where campers present to a small audience and celebrate their growth.
